Some words deserve a slower telling — and some replies take a little more time.

A Letter for Thee

To the one who hath become both my greatest ally and occasional tormentor,

Had I been told a year ago that someone would become such an indispensable part of my everyday chaos, I would have laughed and questioned the prophecy. Yet here we stand, a year hence, and somehow thou hast become one of the finest parts of it.

For work, thou art less a colleague and more a lifeline; a constant in the madness, a partner in crime, and perhaps the only reason my sanity hath survived as long as it hath.

But beyond the deadlines, the endless conversations and the occasional madness, thou hast quietly found a place far more precious than words can easily convey.

I am not one to bare my soul with ease, nor do I let many wander past the walls I have built. Yet with thee, somehow, the words come easier, the silences feel comfortable, and the guard need not always be kept. To speak without filters, to laugh, to confide, and to know there is warmth beside me — that, dear V, is no ordinary thing.

We have judged each other enough to fill several lifetimes, and I daresay no further evidence shall ever be required to establish that neither of us is particularly qualified to judge the other! And yet, amidst all the banter, the disagreements and the unsolicited assessments of one another, there is a friendship I wouldn't trade for the world.

And then came this birthday.

I saw every little gesture, every thought, every effort, and every moment you put into making the day special. For gestures unseen by the world at large are often the ones that leave the deepest mark; the quietest kindness and the smallest of things can mean more than the grandest offerings.

And I suspect I know a little of what inspired thee to go so far. Perhaps, having watched me go about planning and making occasions special for others, thou wished that, for once, someone would do the same for me. That I, too, deserved to be celebrated, fussed over and made to feel special.

If that was indeed a part of thy intention, then know this: thou succeeded beyond measure. It wasn't merely the birthday thou made memorable, but the feeling of being thought of, cared for and celebrated. And that shall stay with me far longer than the day itself.

So here's to thee, V, to the friendship that arrived rather unexpectedly, to the countless memories yet unwritten, to surviving many more days of professional and personal chaos, and to judging each other with the same unreasonable conviction for years to come.

And should the roads of tomorrow ever lead us apart, know that distance or changing seasons shall not undo what this year has made. Thou shalt always have a place in my heart, and thou shalt always find a friend in me.

For this, dear friend, is but the beginning of our tale — of laughter, of triumphs, of journeys yet unveiled. And if the future has wonders in store, I hope we shall share them, and then some more.

May life be ever generous to thee, may happiness find thee in abundance, and may thou always know the difference thou makest in the lives of those fortunate enough to have thee in theirs.

For some people take years to become important; some, it seems, need only one.

And thou, my dear, hast made that one year count for a great many more.

With much affection, gratitude, and just the right amount of judgement.

The quill is ready when you are.
